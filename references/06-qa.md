# 06 QA: prove it before you hand it over

Run these before the long render and again on the final. A step that fails is fixed and rerun, not explained away.

## 1. The words

`build_timeline.py` already refuses a plan that skips, adds or reorders a spoken word. After it passes, read the
phrases back against the transcript once more for names: a misheard product name passes the check if you typed it
the way Whisper heard it. Fix it with `word_fixes`.

## 2. Stills on every beat

```bash
python3 tools/render.py stills 30 95 140 212 380
```

Pick output frames (seconds x 30) on and just after each beat, on each cut, and on the last second. Open them
(`<work>/qa/stills/fNNNN.png`) and look for:

- the graphic landing on its word, not before it;
- text that is cut off, overlapping another element, or unreadable against a bright background;
- the person's edge: a halo, a missing ear or hand, a matte leak onto a lamp or a mic arm
  (`python3 tools/build_base.py preview N` shows source frame N's cut-out over the room, without graphics);
- the room: in plate mode, the plate must sit still behind the person (if it swims, the take was not on a tripod;
  switch to `"live"`).

## 3. Face check

```bash
python3 tools/render.py facecheck        # every 3rd frame; add a number for another step
```

Fails when a caption block, or solid graphics, sit on the face (brow to upper lip) for more than 0.2 s while the
face is shown whole. It prints the phrases and the time spans, and the fix to try first: caption position, camera
framing, or moving a graphic in `beats.js`.

## 4. Safe zone check

```bash
python3 tools/render.py safecheck 3      # every 3rd frame while iterating
python3 tools/render.py safecheck        # every frame, on the final
```

Renders the graphics and captions alone on transparency and fails if any ink (alpha over 24, which includes
shadows) leaves x 65 to 1015, y 269 to 1248. Instagram covers the rest with its own interface.

## 5. Sound

`build_audio.py` prints two lines. Both must read about **-14 LUFS** and a true peak **at or under -1 dBTP**:

```
voice only    -14.1 LUFS  true peak  -1.5 dBTP  11.700 s
voice + sfx   -14.0 LUFS  true peak  -1.5 dBTP  11.700 s
```

A warning about a join too close to an edge means a cut sits within 7 ms of the trim; move the cut. Listen to the
master once on a phone speaker: the voice must stay on top of every sound effect.

## 6. The deliverables

`deliver.py` writes the master, a voice-only version, the cover frame and a contact sheet (one frame per second).
Look at the contact sheet first: it shows a black frame, a frozen stretch or a missing graphic at a glance. Then
watch the master end to end once, at full screen, on a phone if you can.

## What to report to the user

The deliverable paths, the two loudness lines, "facecheck and safecheck passed" (with the step used), and every
judgement call: a word you fixed, a cut that clips a breath, a beat you left out, anything you were unsure about.
